Support

We usually reply within a couple of days.

Get help or report a problem

The fastest route is the app itself: Account → Contact us sends your message straight to us, attached to your account so we can help without back-and-forth.

You can also email admin.marboot@gmail.com.

Get your mosque on Marboot

If your mosque isn't listed yet, request it from inside the app (Account → Request a mosque) with the mosque's name and address, or email us the details. Mosque administrations that want to manage their own listing — publish iqamah times, events and announcements — should mention that in the request and we'll set up administrator access.

Managing your account

  • Change password: Account → Change password.
  • Notification preferences: per-mosque settings live behind the gear icon on each mosque's page; an overview is under Account → Notifications.
  • Delete your account: Account → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and personal data, as described in the privacy policy.
