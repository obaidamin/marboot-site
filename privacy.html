Privacy Policy

Effective August 19, 2026 · Marboot is published by Raha Labs LLC

Marboot connects mosques with the people who follow them. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. The short version: we collect the minimum needed to run the service, we don't show ads, and we never sell your data.

Information you give us

  • No account required. You can use Marboot as a guest — browse mosques, follow them, pin events and set reminders — without giving us your name or email. Creating an account simply keeps your setup when you switch devices.
  • Account details. If you create an account: an email address and password, or your Google or Apple account, to sign in. You may optionally add your first and last name.
  • Your choices in the app. The mosques you follow, notification preferences, pinned events, event reminders and display preferences — stored so they work across your devices.
  • Feedback. Messages you send through the in-app contact form.

Information handled on your device

  • Location. Used on your device to sort nearby mosques and events by distance and to point the qibla compass. Your location is not stored on our servers.
  • Calendar. Only touched when you choose to add an event to your calendar.
  • Photos. Only for mosque administrators who choose a flyer image to import events from. The image is processed to extract event details.
  • Appearance settings such as colour theme stay on your device.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a device push token so mosques you follow can reach you with prayer-time changes, events and announcements. Delivery uses Apple, Google and Expo notification services. You can turn notifications off per mosque in the app or entirely in your device settings.

What mosque administrators can see

When you follow a mosque, that mosque's administrators see their follower count and, at most, an abbreviated display name — your first name and last initial, if you added a name to your account. They never see your email address, your location, your other followed mosques, or any other activity. Administrators cannot edit your account in any way; the only action available to them is removing a follower from their list, which notifies you.

Services we rely on

Marboot runs on Google Firebase (authentication, database and cloud functions) and uses Expo's notification service to deliver pushes. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments. We use no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app (Account → Delete my account, or Mosque Settings → Delete my account for administrators). Deletion removes your account, follows, preferences, pins, reminders and device tokens. If you signed in with Apple, deleting also revokes Marboot's access to your Apple account, so nothing is left connected on Apple's side. Content a mosque administration published to its community (schedules, events, announcements) belongs to the mosque and remains.

Children

Marboot is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and note the new effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us through the in-app contact form or see the support page.
