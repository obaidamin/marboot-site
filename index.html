Stay Informed. Stay Connected.

Marboot connects mosques with the communities around them — prayer times, events and announcements, all in one place.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

Follow the mosques you pray at and their day comes to you — accurate times, upcoming events and urgent announcements, the moment they're published.

🕌

Prayer & iqamah times

Daily adhan and each mosque's own iqamah times, with Friday (Jummah) khutbah schedules. Adhan can be calculated automatically for the mosque's exact location and jurisprudence.

🧭

Qibla compass

A live compass that points to the Kaaba from wherever you are, with the exact bearing spelled out — and it works offline.

📅

Events & reminders

Classes, programs and community events — including weekly and monthly series. Pin the ones you care about and set reminders minutes, hours or days ahead.

📣

Announcements & alerts

Regular announcements and urgent alerts from your mosque's administration reach your phone in seconds.

🔔

Adhan & iqamah alerts

Per-mosque notifications at adhan time and a reminder before iqamah, tuned to how many minutes you need to get there.

👐

Made for every eye

Colour themes including high-contrast modes, adjustable card sizes, and a landscape big-type view of prayer times for readers who want larger text.

For mosque administrations

Marboot gives your mosque a live channel to its community. Publish iqamah and Jummah times once — adhan calculates itself daily. Post events and announcements, send urgent alerts, manage co-administrators, and see your followers. If your mosque isn't on Marboot yet, request it from inside the app or contact us.

A prayer-times display for your wall. Run Marboot on any screen in the masjid — a tablet, a TV stick, an old phone — and it becomes a full-screen board: today's adhan and iqamah times with the next prayer highlighted, Jummah, a rotating panel of upcoming events, and your announcements scrolling along the bottom. It updates the moment you change a time from your phone, chooses its own light or dark look for the room it's in, and looks after itself overnight.
