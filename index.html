For the community

Everything your mosque publishes, on your phone.

Accurate times, upcoming events and urgent announcements from the mosques you follow — with no account required to start.

Prayer & iqamah times

Daily adhan calculated for each mosque’s exact location and method, beside the mosque’s own iqamah times and every Friday (Jummah) congregation.

Today, this week, this month

Flip a mosque’s page from Today to Week or Month. Adhan is calculated for every day; iqamah shows what the mosque has published — and says so plainly when it hasn’t yet.

Adhan & iqamah alerts

Per-mosque notifications at adhan time and a reminder before iqamah, tuned to how many minutes you need to get there. When a mosque changes its iqamah, you hear the day before.

Events & reminders

Classes, programs and community events, including weekly and monthly series. Pin the ones you care about, set reminders minutes, hours or days ahead, and add them to your calendar.

Announcements & alerts

Regular announcements and urgent alerts from your mosque’s administration reach your phone in seconds — and any announcement or event shares as a ready-made card.

Qibla compass

A live compass that points to the Kaaba from wherever you are, with the exact bearing spelled out — and it works offline.

Hijri & Gregorian dates

Both calendars, everywhere dates appear. Each mosque shows its own Hijri date, adjusted by a day when the local moon sighting calls for it.

No account required

Browse mosques, follow them, pin events and set reminders as a guest. Create a free account only when you want your mosques to follow you across devices.

Made for every eye

Color themes including high-contrast and dark looks, and a landscape big-type prayer board for readers who want larger text.

Wall display

A prayer-times board for the masjid — on any screen.

Open a link on any TV browser and it becomes a full-screen board: today’s adhan and iqamah with the next prayer highlighted, every Jumu’ah on its own line, upcoming events, and your announcements scrolling along the bottom. No app to install.

It updates the moment an administrator changes a time from their phone, picks a light or dark look for the room, and dims itself overnight. The same display is built into the app for a tablet or an old phone on the wall.

For mosque administrations

Publish once. Marboot keeps it current.

A live channel from your mosque to its community — and the schedule work done for you.

Adhan time calculation

Adhan is calculated daily for your mosque’s exact location, using the convention your community follows — a dozen to choose from. Prefer your own table? Enter the times yourself.

Iqamah schedule rules

Set your policy once — a fixed time, or “adhan plus 10, rounded to the quarter hour, changing on Sundays” — with seasons for Ramadan and daylight-saving handled automatically. Marboot generates the month, updates itself every night, and tells followers the day before a change.

Wall display

Your mosque’s board at a link — see it above. Share it with whoever runs the TV; it needs nothing but a browser.

Events from a flyer

Pick a flyer photo and Marboot reads the title, date, time and place into an event for you to confirm. Weekly and monthly series are one entry.

Announcements & urgent alerts

Post announcements and send urgent alerts that reach every follower’s phone in seconds. Old items retire themselves on the schedule you set.

Co-administrators & followers

Invite fellow administrators, see how many people follow the mosque (names only, never emails), and manage everything from your phone.
